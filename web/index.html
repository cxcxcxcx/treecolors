Tree Species Color Map

Explore Fall Color Trees

This map highlights deciduous trees, evergreens, and ginkgo across major US and Canadian cities so you can scout neighborhoods for vibrant fall foliage.

The colors mirror the legend: rich greens for year-round foliage, golden hues for ginkgo, and warm reds and purples for species that shine in autumn. Browns and dark tones mark other deciduous trees.

Tap any tree marker to see species details, common names, and curated Wikipedia photos to help you recognize each tree in the real world.
