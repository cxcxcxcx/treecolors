About this map

6,642,191 trees from 40 municipal inventories across the United States and Canada, drawn from each city's open data portal.

Colours follow the legend: green for evergreens, a single orange for ginkgo, red-to-purple for species with striking autumn colour, and brown tones for other deciduous trees.

Tap any tree to see its species, common names, a botanical plate, Wikipedia photos and a link to the original inventory record.

The code that builds the map, the city adapters and the species knowledge base are open source on GitHub. If your city publishes a tree inventory, that is where an adapter can be added.
